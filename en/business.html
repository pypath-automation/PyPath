Peace of mind

The two questions every director asks.

Who maintains the scripts when someone leaves?

The library lives in your own folder, in readable Python with explanations. Everyone who has done PyPath can read and change it. That is easier to hand over than a large Dynamo graph only its maker understands.

So start with two people instead of one: then the knowledge never sits with a single person.

Is it safe for our models?

Practice happens on a simulated model in the app, never on your projects. PyPath collects no data and works offline.

In Revit a script only does what it says, and changes go through a transaction you can undo. pyRevit is free and open source; your IT department decides whether it is installed.

Alongside what you already have

A complement, not a replacement.

PyPath does not replace a building services package such as Stabicad or MagiCAD, nor Dynamo. It is for what your package does not do: your own checks, schedules and house rules.

  • Your own model checksPipes without a system, empty parameters, open ends: checked the same way every time.
  • Your own schedulesLengths, counts and material lists exactly the way you want them, including to Excel.
  • Bulk workStatus, comments or numbers on hundreds of elements at once.
  • House rulesYour own conventions captured in a button everyone can use.

Learning to operate Revit itself is done with a training provider; PyPath is the next step. Stabicad, MagiCAD and Dynamo are trademarks of their owners; PyPath is not affiliated with them.

Costs and reimbursement

Less than one training day per employee.

  • PyPath Basis: €99.99 one-time, the full 12-week plan.
  • PyPath Complete: €249.99 one-time, Basis with the three trade packs (coming soon).
  • For comparison: one classroom training day usually costs €600 to €1,100 excluding VAT in the Netherlands, and your employee is away from work that day.

Reimbursement. In the Netherlands an employer can reimburse training tax-free under the work-related costs scheme (werkkostenregeling, the targeted exemption for training). The employee buys PyPath in the App Store and claims the expense.

Invoice and certificate of participation. App Store purchases are per Apple ID; buying in-app purchases in bulk through Apple is not possible. An invoice in the employer's name and a certificate of participation will come with the team licence, which is in preparation.

PyPath is made by someone from building services engineering for greenhouse horticulture who works with Revit. That is why the exercises are about pipes, greenhouses and facades rather than textbook examples. PyPath is independent of Autodesk and of the pyRevit project.
