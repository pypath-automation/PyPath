What makes PyPath different

Practise with the real Revit API. Without Revit.

In PyPath you write the same lines as in pyRevit: FilteredElementCollector, categories, parameters and transactions. The app runs them on a simulated model of a complete greenhouse. So you can practise on the go what you will use at the office, and nothing can go wrong in a real project.

The same names as in Revit

What you type in PyPath is what you will type in pyRevit. No made-up practice language, but the classes and methods you will find in the Revit API.

Errors like the real thing

Safe to practise

The model is simulated. You cannot break anything, and you do not need a Revit licence to practise.

How it works

Learn by doing. A little every day.

No hour-long videos. A short explanation, then you type it yourself, and PyPath checks it right away.

  1. 1

    Read the exercise

    At most ten minutes of explanation in short blocks, with examples from building services and greenhouse construction. Then you know exactly what your code should do.

  2. 2

    Type it yourself

    A real code editor, with an extra key row for brackets, colons and indentation. You learn by typing, not by watching.

  3. 3

    Get instant feedback

    If something is off, PyPath tells you in plain language what is missing and gives you a hint. No spoilers: you can see the solution after three attempts.

  4. 4

    Practise on the model

    Your code looks for elements in a simulated greenhouse, just like in Revit with pyRevit. You see straight away what it finds.

FAQ

Good to know

Is the app in English?

Not yet. The lessons and the app are in Dutch for now; English lessons are on the roadmap. The code itself uses the real English names from Python, the Revit API and pyRevit.

Do I need programming experience?

No. You start at your very first line of Python. Every lesson explains what you need in short pieces and has you type it yourself right away. After three months you write your own pyRevit tools.

Do I need Revit to practise?

No. PyPath has a simulated Revit environment with the real names from the Revit API, on a model of a complete greenhouse. So you practise on your iPhone or Mac. For your own tools at work, you later use Revit with the free pyRevit add-in on a Windows PC.

Does it work alongside Stabicad, MagiCAD or Dynamo?

Yes. PyPath does not replace a building services package or Dynamo. It complements them: for what your package does not do, and for your own checks and schedules. If you know Dynamo you have a good foundation; with Python and pyRevit you make buttons your whole team can use.

Can I use this at work?

You practise in the app, without Revit and without installing anything on your work PC. To use your tools in Revit later, you need pyRevit: free and open source, but ask your IT department whether you may install it.

Can my employer pay for it?

Yes. In the Netherlands an employer can reimburse training tax-free under the work-related costs scheme (werkkostenregeling). You buy it yourself in the App Store and claim the expense. Use our ready-made letter for your manager.

Will my library work in real Revit?

That is what it is for: the exercises use the real Revit API names, and pyRevit loads your own library automatically. Exporting to a pyRevit extension and testing in real Revit are still to come; until then we say "coming soon".

Do I learn the same Python as in pyRevit?

You learn Python 3. pyRevit uses IronPython 2.7 by default and can also run Python 3. PyPath explains the differences you will run into in practice, such as f-strings versus .format().

Is it a subscription?

No, everything is a one-time purchase. Week 1 is free. After that you buy PyPath Basis (weeks 2 to 12) and, if you like, trade packs for your field, or everything at once with PyPath Complete. What you buy stays yours.

Does it work on iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes. It is one app for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later) and Mac (macOS 15 or later). Your progress syncs via iCloud, and a purchase works on all your devices with the same Apple ID.

What happens to my data?

Nothing. PyPath does not collect any personal data: no account, no tracking, no analytics. Your code runs inside the app, and your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.

Is PyPath made by Autodesk or pyRevit?

No. PyPath is an independent app. Revit belongs to Autodesk, and pyRevit is a free open-source project. PyPath teaches you to work with both.
