Water

Irrigation water, basins and pipe networks: your own tools for water installations, practised on a simulated greenhouse complex with an irrigation unit, distribution pipes and drip lines.

Who it is for

Drafters and engineers who draw water installations: irrigation in greenhouse horticulture, or pipe networks in other buildings.

Tools you build

  • A material list per diameter, ready for ordering
  • Count drip lines and add up their length per section
  • Find open ends and missing end caps
  • Check that every pipe is in the right system
  • Lists to CSV for Excel

The final list comes with the lessons.
