How a trade pack works

  • On top of the basics. A trade pack builds on PyPath Basis: you need the Python basics and the Revit API from the 12-week plan.
  • Lessons and exercises from the trade, on a simulated model: a greenhouse complex with heating and irrigation, or a complete Venlo greenhouse with facades and roof.
  • Your own library. You bundle the functions you write into a library that you later use as a pyRevit extension in real Revit. Exporting it and testing in real Revit is still to come (coming soon).
  • €79.99 one-time per pack, or PyPath Complete (€249.99): Basis with the three launch packs. You then save €89.97.

The trade packs are in development. They go on sale once they have lessons; until then they are marked "coming soon", in the app too. The lessons are in Dutch for now.
